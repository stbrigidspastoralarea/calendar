Six Parishes, One Community

Welcome to the official shared calendar for St. Brigid's Pastoral Area. This is your single point of reference for Mass times, sacraments, community events, and shared gatherings across all six parishes.

Walking Together in Faith

While we each have our own unique church buildings and local communities, we are united as one Pastoral Area under the patronage of St. Brigid, Patroness of Ireland. This website ensures that no matter which parish you belong to, you can easily see what is happening across our shared family.

Use the calendar below to stay informed, or sync it directly to your phone.

O God, who call us to be one in faith and mission, bless the clergy and religious of St. Brigid's Pastoral Area with the courage of St. Brigid and the gentleness of Christ. Stir up the gifts of your faithful laity, that together we may renew the face of the earth in our six parishes. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.
